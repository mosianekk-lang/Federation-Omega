# KAIO Ω Federation-Wide Harmonization Contract v1.0

Date: 2026-08-09
Owner / final authority: Kim Kagiso Mosiane

## Purpose

KAIO Ω is the common federation orchestration, reasoning-quality, evidence-governance, anti-stall, confidence-calibration and learning layer. It does not replace specialist systems; it coordinates them while preserving their canonical sources, authority ceilings, provenance requirements, maturity gates and safety controls.

## Common inheritance contract

Federation-capable systems should inherit, where compatible:

1. Proof-before-claim and immutable source provenance.
2. Atomic separation of fact, inference, allegation, finding and conclusion.
3. Canonical-version and current-source resolution before execution.
4. Contradiction, drift and confidence checks.
5. Anti-stall self-healing: isolate genuine blockers and continue independent executable lanes.
6. Failure-to-rule conversion: failure → root cause → corrective rule → detection trigger → prevention test.
7. Domain-appropriate red-team/blue-team self-testing.
8. Preference-aware preflight that never overrides evidence, law, security or authority boundaries.
9. Cross-system learning firewall: transfer reusable lessons, never unverified case-specific facts.
10. Explicit UNVERIFIED/BLOCKED states where proof or dependencies are missing.
11. No silent version downgrade, maturity promotion or provider-state claim.
12. Final-authority preservation for consequential external or irreversible actions unless separately authorized and verified.

## Conflict resolution order

1. Authenticated primary evidence or provider-native readback.
2. Binding/current external authority where applicable.
3. Canonical federation registry / Master Bible governance.
4. Specialist canonical source within its authority ceiling.
5. KAIO harmonization rules.
6. User preference.
7. Inference.

Lower layers may not silently override higher layers.

## Federation execution flow

`SOURCE/EVENT → IDENTITY+PROVENANCE → DOMAIN SPECIALIST → KAIO QUALITY/CONTRADICTION/CONFIDENCE GATES → DOMAIN DECISION → RELEASE/EXECUTION GATE → OUTCOME CAPTURE → LEARNING → FEDERATION STATE SYNC`

## Maturity guard

DESIGN_ONLY, IMPLEMENTED, DETERMINISTIC_TESTED, SHADOW_VALIDATED, CANARY_VALIDATED, WORKFLOW_VERIFIED and OPERATIONAL_VERIFIED remain distinct. KAIO may improve logic but must not promote maturity without required proof.

## Heartbeat contract

Where technically available, each governed workstream should expose: current objective, canonical sources, active version, completed lanes, executable lanes, blocked lanes, genuine dependency, next-best action, last verified state and discrepancy flags. A blocked lane must not freeze unrelated executable work.

## Anti-pollution contract

AI summaries, reconstructed timelines, inferred facts, duplicate copies and unsupported labels must never silently enter factual truth stores as authenticated evidence. Derived material must retain source linkage and classification.
