# SLOS Hyperperformance Convergence v1

Status: SOURCE_IMPLEMENTED_PENDING_ADMISSION

## Objective

Convert Superior Logic from conversational orchestration into a deterministic mission-compilation and empirical route-selection control system without expanding provider authority.

## Parallel lanes

1. MissionIR compiler: objective -> typed DAG -> proof obligations -> hyperparallel waves.
2. Federation digital twin: capability/authority/proof/cost/risk graph with deterministic route synthesis.
3. Shadow evolution: champion/challenger evaluation on common real missions only; promotion requires evidence floor and measurable gain.
4. Evidence distillation: immutable-source digest + bounded control-plane receipt.
5. Admission lane: regression and ProofOS/Airlock remain sovereign over source promotion.

## Hyperperformance invariants

- Independent DAG nodes may execute in the same wave.
- Dependency ordering cannot be bypassed for speed.
- Irreversible nodes require a non-internal authority class.
- Low-utility nodes may be suppressed only when they are not required dependencies.
- Route synthesis never grants authority; it selects only among capabilities already represented in the digital twin.
- Unavailable, over-risk, under-proof or authority-incompatible routes are excluded before ranking.
- Challenger promotion uses common-mission evidence to prevent evaluation-set gaming.
- Shadow evolution never dispatches effects.
- Raw evidence is not copied into the control plane when a cryptographically bound compact receipt is sufficient.
- A receipt cannot verify altered raw evidence.

## Non-claims

This release does not claim provider-live deployment, autonomous external mutation, production cutover, distributed consensus, AGI, market superiority or automatic stable promotion.
