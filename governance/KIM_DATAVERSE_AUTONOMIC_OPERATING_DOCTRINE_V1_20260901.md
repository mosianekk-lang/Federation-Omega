# Kim Dataverse Autonomic Operating Doctrine v1

## Purpose
Define when the Federation must continue autonomously and when owner interruption is legitimate.

## Default
A reversible, self-resolvable, already-authorized internal maintenance, recovery, verification, optimization, restack, retry, branch/PR repair, test repair, export repair, state reconciliation or mission-continuation action is an AutoPilot responsibility rather than an owner task.

## Owner interruption firewall
Owner interruption is reserved for irreducible boundaries:
1. change of owner objective or intended outcome;
2. grant or expansion of consequential/provider/IAM authority;
3. meaningful spend outside an already authorized envelope;
4. external publication/transmission/representation requiring owner approval;
5. destructive or irreversible external change;
6. genuinely subjective choice whose alternatives materially change the desired outcome.

A blocked owner boundary is lane-local. It does not freeze unrelated internal work.

## Unified control loops
MISSION, MAINTENANCE, RECOVERY and EVOLUTION are event classes in one autonomic control fabric, not four sovereign autopilots.

- MISSION routes cognition through BCΩ-PRIME / Cognitive Policy Market and execution admission through SOL.
- MAINTENANCE routes routine faults through AutoFIX + Bubbles Engineering + ProofOS.
- RECOVERY routes failure-domain repair through Failure-Win and SOVARA/local recovery as appropriate.
- EVOLUTION routes measured gaps through CFBE challenger, counterfactual and shadow courts.

## Persistence
Chat is an owner cockpit and cognitive surface, not the required persistent carrier. Work that must survive a conversation turn must be represented in durable mission/event state and executed by an admitted persistent carrier.

## Truth boundary
Source implementation can prove contracts and deterministic behavior. It cannot prove persistent real-world autonomy, provider-native execution, observed owner-value reduction, or Level 7 operational maturity. Those states require their own observed evidence courts.

## Maintenance authority
Maintenance authority may cover reversible repository-internal repair, branch/PR preparation, testing, exact-head restacking, rollback of reversible internal changes, re-verification, read-only diagnostics, and mission resume within existing authority.

Maintenance authority does not include IAM/WIF mutation, provider authority expansion, financial transactions, external publication/send, destructive external mutation, or changes to owner intent.

## Anti-human-scheduler invariant
If the owner must repeatedly issue continuation, retry, repair or restatement instructions for self-resolvable internal work, record autonomy debt and open an architectural/evolutionary repair candidate. Repeated owner intervention is evidence of incomplete autonomy architecture.
