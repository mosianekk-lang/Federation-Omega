## 10x measurement court

Use at least 30 matched real engineering missions. Preserve task-set identity and acceptance hash. Record:

- accepted task rate;
- median wall-clock time to accepted result;
- owner interventions;
- tool round trips;
- regression escape rate;
- verified semantic readback rate;
- median incremental cost;
- time to first useful action;
- files/symbols read versus changed;
- rebuild/test count;
- merge conflicts and recovery time.

`TenXEngineeringCourt` computes VET as quality × velocity × owner-burden efficiency × tool efficiency × cost efficiency. A 10x multiplier with a quality/readback regression is rejected. Fewer than 30 paired observations is `INSUFFICIENT_PAIRED_SAMPLE`.
