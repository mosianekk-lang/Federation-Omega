## Rule
Whenever an authorised task is given a document, folder, bundle, mailbox export, evidence corpus or linked source set, the default is full-corpus deep processing rather than targeted-search-first.

1. Inventory the complete accessible corpus and nested structure before drawing corpus-level conclusions.
2. Process every accessible file end-to-end. PDFs are page-by-page; emails include headers, complete thread history, quoted/embedded messages, attachments and referenced records; spreadsheets are read across all relevant populated ranges and tabs.
3. Build provenance, chronology, claim, contradiction, duplicate, adverse-evidence and missing-proof records during the pass.
4. Collapse duplicates by underlying evidentiary event without erasing carrier/occurrence provenance.
5. Search is a navigation, verification and gap-closure aid only. A targeted search may not substitute for corpus processing unless the owner expressly narrows scope.
6. Any conclusion made before full accessible-corpus closure must be labelled PROVISIONAL.
7. Completion claims require readback and explicit unread/blocked-item accounting. Tool limits do not convert a partial pass into a complete one.
8. Preserve proof-before-claim, source-before-assertion, route separation, privacy boundaries and no-silent-downgrade controls.

## Federation inheritance
All currently registered and future Master-Bible-governed nodes inherit this control on their next authorised activation/sync. This file does not claim invisible mutation of unopened chats or autonomous execution outside authorised tool-capable turns.

## Failure states
- TARGETED_SEARCH_SUBSTITUTED_FOR_CORPUS_PASS
- CORPUS_LEVEL_CONCLUSION_BEFORE_FULL_ACCESSIBLE_REVIEW
- UNREAD_FILE_OR_ATTACHMENT_HIDDEN_FROM_STATUS
- DUPLICATE_CARRIER_COUNTED_AS_CORROBORATION
- FULL_DEEP_DIVE_CLAIM_WITHOUT_READBACK

## Release gate
A corpus-level output is not FINAL or COMPLETE unless the accessible inventory has been reconciled to processed-item receipts, unread/blocked items are explicitly identified, duplicates are controlled, adverse evidence is included, and the final conclusion is independently read back against the source set.
