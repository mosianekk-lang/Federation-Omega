# CFBE Deep Market Harvest — AEGIS-Ω v0.3.0

Current public market capabilities were decomposed into primitives rather than copied as product branding. The harvest includes Google Android Advanced Protection and Intrusion Logging; Apple Lockdown Mode and threat notifications; Microsoft Defender for Endpoint mobile MTD and Android MTD Role; CrowdStrike Falcon for Mobile; Zimperium MTD; Check Point Harmony Mobile; Amnesty MVT/AndroidQF; and current Citizen Lab mercenary-spyware research.

1. Single-toggle high-risk hardening and anti-disablement.
2. Privacy-preserving forensic observability and owner-controlled log access.
3. On-device behavioral/anomaly detection for emerging threats.
4. Enterprise risk signals feeding conditional access.
5. Cross-platform unified alerting and centralized case visibility.
6. App/network/OS/file coverage rather than single-vector scanning.
7. Threat-family-independent behavioral evidence plus classical indicators.
8. Consensual reproducible forensic collection and evidence lineage.
9. Mobile risk scoring without routine content inspection.
10. Dynamic protection-rule refresh with bounded rollout.
11. Resilient platform-level security-role integration where the OS permits it.
12. Investigation/containment separation with human control over consequential response.

## Cold-slate AEGIS composition

AEGIS combines those genes with capabilities that are normally separate: a poisoning-resistant evidence graph, tamper-evident evidence chains, SOL 6.2 transactional idempotency/outbox recovery, synthetic adversarial digital twin, shadow neural challenger, offline evolutionary policy tournament, proof-gated provider deployment and a comparable-baseline 10× court.

The build remains defensive and clean-room. Publicly observable capabilities and open standards inform the design; proprietary source, exploit chains, private indicators, credentials and vendor internals are not copied or extracted.
