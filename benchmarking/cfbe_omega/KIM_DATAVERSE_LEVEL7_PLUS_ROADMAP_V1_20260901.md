## Maturity ladder
### Level 5 — Institutional Intelligence
Exit criteria:
- objective ecology exists and identifies shared dependencies/conflicts;
- resource allocation is explicit and bounded;
- owner interruption budget defaults to zero for self-resolvable internal work;
- autonomy debt is measured;
- dynamic work-cell topology is selected per mission;
- digital twin reports actual proof maturity, not architecture claims.

### Level 6 — Self-Evolving Institution
Exit criteria:
- candidate policies and architectures are generated from measured gaps;
- historical replay + adversarial + shadow qualification is mandatory;
- architectural entropy controller can recommend merge/retire/convert-to-policy decisions;
- causal learning records mechanism hypotheses and falsification state;
- no self-promotion to consequential authority.

### Level 7 — Sovereign Digital Organization
Exit criteria:
- persistent mission continuity no longer depends on an open chat turn;
- maintenance, recovery, mission and evolution operate as one unified autonomic fabric with distinct event classes;
- owner is interrupted only for irreducible intent/authority/economic/external-representation/destructive decisions;
- verified outcome and value telemetry govern capability retention;
- provider and mission failures remain lane-local unless independently proven systemic;
- the system can reorganize its workforce and route portfolio automatically within existing authority.

### Level 8+ — Evidence-Governed Adaptive Institution
Not a claim of current maturity. Candidate frontier beyond Level 7:
- multi-timescale objective portfolio optimization;
- constitutional policy amendments qualified by independent courts;
- capability market with retirement pressure and reuse incentives;
- cross-provider counterfactual route simulation;
- information-value budgeting;
- institution-wide negative-knowledge / failure-genome diffusion;
- explicit option-value preservation under uncertainty;
- owner-attention leverage as a first-class outcome metric.
