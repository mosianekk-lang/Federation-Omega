# Kim Dataverse Level 7+ CFBE Benchmark v1

Status: SOURCE / CONTROL-PLANE BENCHMARK. This is not a claim of Level 7 operational maturity.

## Benchmark method
The benchmark evaluates the currently implemented Federation architecture against the Level 5–8 institutional-intelligence target. Each dimension is classified as one of:

- REUSE — an existing capability already supplies most of the required primitive;
- COMPOSE — existing capabilities exist but require a cross-system composition layer;
- BUILD — a missing bounded primitive must be added;
- PROVIDER — source can be prepared but native runtime/readback is required;
- VALUE — prospective observed owner-value evidence is required.

## Current maturity interpretation

### Level 5 — Institutional Intelligence
Source maturity: **candidate / partially implemented**.

Strong existing prerequisites: SOL 6.2, BCΩ-PRIME/Cognitive Policy Market, Bubbles dynamic work allocation, SOVARA provider isolation, ProofOS independent courts, CFBE reuse-first benchmarking, durable mission/event primitives, and Failure-Win/AutoFIX recovery.

New source primitives introduced in this tranche: Objective Ecology, Resource Economy, Autonomy Debt Index, Owner Interruption Firewall, architecture entropy recommendation, owner-attention leverage, and sequential Level 5–8 maturity evaluation.

Operational Level 5 remains unclaimed until integrated observed mission episodes prove the combined behavior.

### Level 6 — Self-Evolving Institution
Source maturity: **strong prerequisites, composition incomplete**.

Existing evolution governors, shadow campaigns, causal/counterfactual tools, challenger markets, regression and rollback courts materially reduce new-build need. Missing pieces are institutional composition, constitutional amendment qualification and estate-wide architectural entropy feedback.

### Level 7 — Sovereign Digital Organization
Operational maturity: **not yet proven**.

The decisive missing evidence is not more architecture. It is persistent no-chat execution, observed autonomous maintenance/recovery, owner-interruption reduction, provider-native readback where claimed, and sustained prospective value.

### Level 8+ — Evidence-Governed Adaptive Institution
Frontier candidate only. No operational claim.

## Upgrade strategy
The highest-leverage path is composition over invention:

1. install institutional control primitives;
2. bind them to existing PRIME/SOL/Bubbles/SOVARA/ProofOS capabilities;
3. turn routine maintenance failures into autonomic events;
4. project a digital twin from proof-bearing state;
5. measure autonomy debt and owner-attention leverage on real missions;
6. qualify persistent carriers and provider-native wait/wake/readback;
7. populate prospective owner-value cohorts;
8. promote only narrowly proven autonomy capabilities.

## No-go shortcuts
- Do not label source completion as Level 7.
- Do not promote synthetic/shadow episodes into owner-value proof.
- Do not let PRIME, AutoPilot, Bubbles or SOVARA inherit SOL authority.
- Do not mutate IAM/WIF/provider trust as a maintenance shortcut.
- Do not create a second scheduler, memory root or provider executor to achieve persistence.
- Do not globally stall independent work because one provider or authority lane is blocked.
