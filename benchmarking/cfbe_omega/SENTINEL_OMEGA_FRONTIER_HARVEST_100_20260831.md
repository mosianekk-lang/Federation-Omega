## Architecture decision

Do **not** build 100 new bots. Preserve one owner-facing Sentinel Ω identity and dynamically compose bounded specialists.

Target operating model:

`TELEMETRY -> TOPOLOGY -> DEDUP/GROUP -> CAUSAL COURT -> PRECURSOR/SLO RISK -> CFBE ROUTE MARKET -> BUBBLES SPECIALIST SQUAD -> CANARY REPAIR -> PROVIDER READBACK -> REGRESSION/SOAK -> KNOWLEDGE DIFFUSION -> OWNER-TIME VALUE`

## Competitive differentiators Sentinel Ω should retain

The frontier systems are stronger today in telemetry breadth and productized operational integrations. Sentinel Ω should not imitate them by weakening its existing controls. Its differentiators are:

- explicit competing hypotheses and falsification;
- immutable event truth vs current projection vs interpretation;
- action-specific authority rather than generic agent permission;
- independent executor/verifier separation;
- rollback and proof before maturity promotion;
- route competition and anti-churn;
- bounded autonomous repair with owner-reserved consequential authority;
- cross-system learning requiring adoption/execution proof;
- owner attention treated as a scarce resource.
