# EvidenceOps Audio Accuracy Opportunity Register

## Implemented in v3.1

1. **Architecture-family gate** — blocks Whisper-versus-Whisper from being described as independent consensus.
2. **Critical-passage escalation** — prioritises jurisdiction, ruling, bundle, disclosure and other legally material disagreements.
3. **Hearing-specific calibration** — calculates WER and bounded model weights from a human-verified sample.
4. **Quotation release gate** — requires architecture diversity, timestamps, supported role, verified legal entities, human listening and audio-window hash.
5. **Strict evidence lexicon** — corrections require approved exact aliases; broad fuzzy semantic replacement is prohibited.
6. **Ranked review queue** — review order reflects disagreement, architecture diversity, legal criticality, missing alignment and speaker uncertainty.

## Staged opportunities

7. **Stereo-channel audit** — the source metadata records two channels. If left and right differ materially, per-channel recognition may reduce overlap and improve speaker separation. No benefit is claimed until correlation and clipping are measured on the original source.
8. **Original-versus-denoised ensemble** — a conservatively enhanced derivative may contribute a secondary hypothesis but can never replace the original transcript by itself.
9. **Chirp 3 inline case phrases** — use exact case names, numbers and institutional terminology when Google authority becomes available.
10. **Parakeet word boosting** — use the verified case lexicon through NeMo decoding customisation when supported by the selected runtime.
11. **OpenAI dual-output lane** — use `gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize` for speaker segments and `gpt-4o-transcribe` for lexical log probabilities; both remain one architecture family.
12. **Gold-window programme** — manually verify 12 critical windows totalling 1,231 seconds and use measured WER to calibrate model weights.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- treating different checkpoint sizes from the same family as independent evidence;
- silent legal-term correction;
- replacing original evidence with denoised audio;
- biometric speaker naming without a separately authorised and reliable process;
- direct quotation from any automated transcript before the quotation gate passes.
