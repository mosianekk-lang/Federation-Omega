Release: v0.1  
Commercial status: Internal release candidate  
External use: Founder approval required before quotation, publication, outreach, contract acceptance or price commitment.

## EvidenceOps value proposition

EvidenceOps converts fragmented evidence, opaque decisions and unreliable workflows into proof-carrying, reviewable and reusable operating systems.

Every engagement is governed by four rules:

1. Evidence before assertion.
2. Authority before execution.
3. Readback before completion.
4. Clear separation between verified capability, conditional capability and future development.

## Offer 1 — Evidence Provenance Passport Sprint

**Buyer problem:** Important files exist, but their integrity, relationship, source lineage and inclusion status are difficult to prove.

**Suitable buyers:** Legal teams, unions, investigators, regulated organisations, governance offices, professional practices and AI-agent operators.

**Core deliverables:**

- bounded corpus manifest;
- cryptographic leaf hashes;
- Merkle root;
- deterministic inclusion proofs;
- proof receipt;
- verification report;
- machine-readable passport;
- handling and limitation note.

**Verified capability basis:** A private five-file Provenance Passport was generated and all five inclusion proofs passed deterministic verification.

**Internal planning price:** ZAR 7,500–25,000 per bounded corpus, subject to volume, sensitivity, source condition and required assurance.

**Not included:** Legal authenticity findings, court admissibility opinions, forensic imaging, external notarisation or public-chain anchoring unless separately contracted.

## Offer 2 — Matter Evidence Sprint

**Buyer problem:** A matter contains large volumes of documents and communications, but the issues, proof, gaps, contradictions, burdens and next actions are not organised into a decision-ready structure.

**Suitable buyers:** Applicants, representatives, legal practices, unions, employee-relations teams, governance units and investigation teams.

**Core deliverables:**

- bounded source register;
- issue-to-evidence matrix;
- chronology;
- contradiction and gap register;
- claim/evidence separation;
- burden and proof map;
- disclosure schedule;
- decision-readiness memorandum;
- proof receipt for the processed scope.

**Internal planning price:** ZAR 18,000–75,000 per sprint.

**Boundary:** This is evidence operations and decision-support work. It is not a substitute for advice from an admitted legal practitioner where legal representation or reserved legal work is required.

## Offer 3 — Workspace Automation Sprint

**Buyer problem:** Work is scattered across Gmail, Drive and Workspace files, with repeated manual handling, weak handoffs and insufficient execution receipts.

**Suitable buyers:** Small and medium organisations, professional practices, founders, governance teams and evidence-intensive operations.

**Core deliverables:**

- workflow and authority map;
- connector and permission assessment;
- proof-gated automation design;
- bounded Google Workspace implementation within verified connector scopes;
- exception and approval gates;
- readback tests;
- operating register;
- recovery and handover pack.

**Verified capability basis:** Authorised Google Drive read/write and document readback are available in the current connected environment.

**Internal planning price:** ZAR 25,000–120,000 per sprint.

**Conditional elements:** Google Cloud, Cloud Run, Secret Manager, Workload Identity Federation and provider-runtime deployment are quoted only after the required identities, scopes, bindings and health readbacks are independently verified.

## Offer 4 — Legal Audio Lab: Evidence Readiness & Provenance Sprint

**Buyer problem:** Audio evidence exists, but files are poorly inventoried, provenance is unclear, transcription readiness is unknown and review cannot begin reliably.

**Suitable buyers:** Legal teams, unions, investigators, disciplinary processes and governance offices.

**Core deliverables:**

- audio corpus inventory;
- file integrity and provenance passport;
- format and quality assessment;
- segmentation plan;
- speaker/issue review framework;
- transcription-provider readiness assessment;
- processing risk register;
- canary execution plan;
- verified handoff receipt.

**Internal planning price:** ZAR 12,000–45,000 per sprint, excluding provider usage, specialist enhancement and transcription fees.

**Current truth boundary:** Live transcription is not offered as an already verified runtime capability. Provider credentials, Google Speech binding and the private cloud worker must pass independent verification before live transcription execution is promised.

## Common commercial controls

- Pricing bands are internal planning values, not approved quotations.
- The Founder remains final authority for external pricing, contract acceptance and public statements.
- Confidential evidence is not placed in public repositories.
- Work is bounded by an agreed corpus, purpose, jurisdiction and authority.
- Every material completion claim requires execution evidence and readback.
- Customer-specific learning and data reuse follow the purchased confidentiality and learning-rights tier.
- Destructive, irreversible or externally consequential actions require explicit approval.
