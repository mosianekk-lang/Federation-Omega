1. The founder-controlled mission is authoritative.
2. Only the founder may materially amend the mission.
3. The mission defines the required capability.
4. Current capability may change the route; it may not silently reduce the outcome.
5. Every substantive input must consider relevant Kim Dataverse intelligence where an authorised route exists.
6. No directive may be treated as isolated where relevant canonical context exists.
7. Every validated directive must be assessed for governed downstream impact, repair, innovation, opportunity and reusable value.
8. Mission Delta remains workforce-owned until verified closure, founder amendment, or exhaustion of authorised routes.
9. Truthful disclosure of incompletion does not discharge ownership of incompletion.
10. A local mirror is not a Kim Dataverse update; a Dataverse write is incomplete until readback and receipt.
11. A plan is not execution; code is not production; a configured trigger is not a verified triggered run.
12. No unresolved burden or harm may be silently transferred to the founder or another affected party.
