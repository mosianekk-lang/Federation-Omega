A source-local audit engine for evidence, governance, compliance, operational and forensic work.

## Core advantages

- zero-copy by default;
- adaptive risk sampling;
- evidence-graph integrity and impact closure;
- contradiction detection;
- authority and proof gates;
- cryptographic Merkle proof bundle;
- adapter-driven source-local operation;
- derived-output-only export model.

The engine is designed to outperform checklist-only audits on source sovereignty, adaptive focus, graph integrity, downstream impact analysis and cryptographic proof. Superiority is not claimed until benchmarked against named alternatives on real datasets.
