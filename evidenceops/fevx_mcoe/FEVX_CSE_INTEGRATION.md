# FEVX CSE placement inside the EvidenceOps ecosystem

## Decision

FEVX CSE is registered as a Federation Omega shared subsystem. EvidenceOps consumes it through `evidenceops_fevx_adapter_v1`; it is not embedded in the EvidenceOps source, fact, chronology, disclosure or bundle kernels.

## Separation of responsibility

| Layer | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Federation Omega | Constitutional ownership, authority, trust and release |
| FEVX CSE | Intent, uncertainty, strategy, competence, simulation and outcome assurance |
| EvidenceOps adapter | Case wall, read-only packet, derived ledger and proof |
| EvidenceOps | Sources, verified facts, claims, chronology, law, procedure and bundles |
| Matter workspace | Matter-specific evidence and owner-reviewed decisions |

## Initial authority

The adapter is supervised, read-only and A0/A1. It has no legal, sending, payment, publication, destructive, data-disclosure, production-deployment, source-write or verified-fact-write authority.

## Trust

Provider proof of the adapter establishes only the synthetic control canary. It does not establish legal accuracy or transfer trust to a real matter. A real matter must be separately case-walled, supervised, measured and owner reviewed.
