# Truth boundary

A successful provider canary proves that the adapter executed the actual 20-module CSE runtime against a synthetic, case-walled packet and enforced its read-only controls.

It does not prove:

- the correctness of legal advice;
- the truth of an unverified claim;
- a favourable case outcome;
- authority to send, file, publish, pay, delete or disclose;
- trust in a different matter;
- Level 6 workflow-specific autonomy.
