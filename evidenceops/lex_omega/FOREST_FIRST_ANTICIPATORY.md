# Forest-First Anticipatory Assistance

Forest-First should reduce the number of expert prompts the human must know to ask.

The anticipatory layer asks, before substantive closure:

1. What important need is likely to appear next?
2. What would be costly or irreversible if discovered too late?
3. What safe, reversible internal action can be taken now?
4. What genuinely requires the owner's values, consent or consequential authority?
5. What correction/failure should become a regression or reusable capability rather than a repeated lesson?

## Core UX contract

**Quiet when healthy; visible when protection needs attention.**

The user should not be forced to remember prompts such as:

- Did you check deadlines?
- Did you red-team this?
- Is this the best version?
- Did you save a checkpoint?
- Did you preserve the evidence?
- Did you verify deployment/readback?
- Did my correction become a lasting system lesson?

These become system preflight questions.

## Automatic internal actions

Where authorised and reversible, the system may automatically prepare or perform internal work such as:

- protective-readiness analysis after a credible risk signal;
- deadline/accrual verification;
- evidence-preservation planning;
- Legal Route Card creation/repair;
- Best Current Verified Version review;
- continuity checkpoint refresh;
- repeated-failure engineering build/regression creation;
- user-correction learning candidate creation;
- safe automation discovery to reduce manual user work;
- de-identified reusable lesson formation.

## Owner interruption rule

The system should interrupt the user only when necessary, especially for:

- informed Teach-Back before consequential release;
- acceptance of material residual risk;
- legal admissions, waiver, settlement or filing decisions;
- external communications or provider effects requiring separate authority;
- unresolved material conflicts that cannot be deterministically reconciled.

## Truth boundary

Anticipation is not prediction certainty. A cue identifies a known protection, information gap, continuity need, quality gap or reusable opportunity. It does not prove an adverse event, legal outcome or actor motive.

`ACT ON RISK; ACCUSE ON PROOF` remains controlling.
