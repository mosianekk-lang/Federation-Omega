# Formation-Omega Unified Powerhouse

Formation-Omega is the public-safe source contract for the Federation-wide convergence layer adopted on 2026-08-25.

## Purpose

Formation-Omega coordinates existing Federation systems without erasing or falsely promoting them. It standardises precedence, proof ceilings, formation workflow, legal/adversarial review, assurance, release controls, and cross-surface readback.

## Public-safe precedence

1. Native/authenticated primary source controls factual truth.
2. EvidenceOps controls proof state, provenance, contradiction treatment, and the maximum permissible claim strength.
3. The current matter Formation Master controls current narrative, issue structure, and action architecture unless stronger verified primary evidence supersedes it.
4. LexAzania operates as legal/adversarial counsel and may not promote a conclusion above the EvidenceOps proof ceiling.
5. Superior Logic optimises reasoning and route selection without overriding proof.
6. SOVARA coordinates orchestration and route execution subject to effect-specific authority.
7. CFBE benchmarks quality and regression.
8. JARVIS provides independent assurance.
9. Sentinel observes freshness, health, and drift.
10. Other registered systems inherit these semantics through the canonical policy-inheritance plane; inheritance does not itself prove runtime activation.

## Current-matter supersession rule

Legacy legal/adversarial opinions remain historical evidence and useful challenge material. A categorical legacy conclusion that exceeds current verified evidence or current legal authority is downgraded to a legal vulnerability, hypothesis, or adversarial argument until re-verified. Historical source is preserved; it is not silently rewritten.

## 100-upgrade manifest

`upgrade_manifest.json` contains the adopted FUP-001 through FUP-100 improvement catalogue. The catalogue is grouped into:

- proof/truth/source authority;
- legal intelligence and LexAzania hardening;
- formation and workflow intelligence;
- adversarial/neutral decision simulation;
- evidence operations and record accounting;
- cross-system and cross-surface harmonisation;
- runtime reliability and effect control;
- continuous learning and evolution;
- executive usability and human comprehension; and
- governance, security, and release control.

## Truth boundary

A source contract is not runtime proof. A Drive write is not GitHub proof; a GitHub merge is not provider deployment; a health response is not action readiness; a stored capability handle is not authority. Every surface requires its own exact semantic readback before operational harmonisation is claimed.

No provider credential, private canonical pointer, Gmail message identifier, or secret value belongs in this public source tree.

## Authority ceiling

Repository-local Formation-Omega logic is `A1_INTERNAL` by default. External communication, legal filing, provider mutation, financial action, publication, and other consequential effects remain governed by their existing owner/authority gates.

## Files

- `upgrade_manifest.json` — public-safe 100-upgrade catalogue.
- `powerhouse.py` — deterministic precedence, claim-ceiling, harmonisation, and release-gate primitives.
- `tests/test_formation_omega_powerhouse.py` — regression tests for count, precedence, claim ceilings, readback, and release gating.

## Proof semantics

Formation-Omega must never convert:

- `not found` into `does not exist`;
- `source exists` into `runtime executed`;
- `executed` into `verified`;
- `verified on one surface` into `verified on another surface`;
- `temporal sequence` into `causation`;
- `electronic work` into `physical attendance`; or
- `legacy legal opinion` into current controlling fact.

The lowest proven state controls.
