## Executive finding

The estate's dominant weakness is **not absence of capability**. It is fragmentation:

1. strong capabilities exist across source, Drive control planes, Canva, Gmail, WIF/GitHub, and local/Windows work;
2. many are not uniformly discoverable by FCOA/Strategic FUSE at runtime;
3. several capabilities are mature in source/control but lack fresh provider-semantic readback;
4. some direct connectors are unavailable even though alternate FUSE routes already exist.

The required architecture is therefore a **cross-estate capability currentness fabric**, not another sovereign controller.

## Maturity labels

- LIVE — current callable connector/provider readback exists.
- LIVE_PARTIAL — a useful route is callable but only some semantics are proven.
- BOUND_PARTIAL — control plane and identity exist, runtime/action proof incomplete.
- SOURCE_ONLY — code/policy exists but live provider execution is not proven.
- PROVIDER_READBACK_REQUIRED — route may exist; freshness/action-specific proof is missing.
- CONNECTOR_UNAVAILABLE — no direct connector in this ChatGPT runtime.
- ADMIN_DISABLED — provider connector exists but is unavailable by administrator policy.
