Frontier Convergence OS (FC-OS) turns the already-merged Superior Logic × Gemini Frontier Convergence suite into a permanent provider-neutral capability-evolution operating layer.

It does **not** replace Superior Logic, SOVARA, CFBE, Formation Omega, the Stage-20 autonomous maturation controller, Failure-Win, CFRE, RealityGuard, ChatBridge or the existing Frontier Convergence runtime. ## New FC-OS capabilities

### Capability Genome

A capability is represented as portable and provider-specific genes with dependencies, proof requirements, portability and reversibility. This lets the system harvest the mechanism behind a vendor feature rather than copy a vendor product wholesale.

### Causal Attribution

A challenger may be credited with an improvement only when the comparison context is stable and the test isolates a single mechanism delta. Different fixtures, authority, observation windows or controls are not treated as causal peers.

### Evidence Economics

Experiments are ranked by expected information gain, mission value, proof gain and reversibility, with penalties for cost, latency, owner burden and risk. Repeating a low-information retry is structurally disfavored.

### Shadow Tournament

Champion and challengers must share a comparison key. Quality, reliability and owner-burden floors are protected. A challenger wins automatically only when it is the unique Pareto-front survivor and produces greater outcome value. Ambiguous Pareto fronts remain held.

### Three evolution modes

- **VERTICAL** — improve a capability within its present boundary.
- **HORIZONTAL** — transfer a mechanism to another receiver. Proof never transfers; the target receiver needs a current capability lease.
- **ARCHITECTURAL** — alter a constitutional system boundary. FC-OS may propose and simulate this, but it may not self-promote it. Explicit owner approval plus proof, simulation, rollback and independent readback are mandatory.

### Chaos / Recovery Court

Safe non-destructive fault injection checks detection, failure-domain isolation, correct degraded state, rollback and collateral-regression absence. Destructive chaos requires separate authority and is rejected by this A1 implementation.

### Value Realization

Before/after value receipts record quality, reliability, latency, cost, owner burden and outcome value. A positive-value claim requires protected floors to remain intact and outcome value to improve.

### Evidence Virtualization

Large logs, research payloads and provider responses remain in the evidence plane. FC-OS returns a content hash, byte count, compact excerpt and storage reference. Raw evidence is not inserted into chat or a single spreadsheet cell by default.

### Bounded Discovery Planner

Provider/tool discovery is partitioned into small probes with explicit result-size limits. This directly prevents recurrence of the `FCG-GEMINI-DISCOVER-20260828-001` failure, where a discovery result exceeded Google Sheets' 50,000-character single-cell ceiling.

### Stage-20 Bridge

Capability genomes become ordinary Stage-20 maturation gaps. This keeps one maturity institution: FC-OS enriches the candidate/experiment layer while the admitted Stage-20 controller retains maturity planning.
