# Superior Logic Convergence Measurement v1

## Purpose

Measure before further Superior Logic architecture growth. This court compares a full-doctrine active-context baseline with a typed constitutional core plus mission-specific control slice and Bubbles mission capsule.

## Reuse-first design

The court creates no scheduler, provider router, trace store, idempotency store, freshness system or payload interceptor. It reuses admitted Bubbles/CFBE components for those concerns and adds only the paired measurement/oracle layer.

## First synthetic corpus

Eight privacy-safe mission classes cover current-state reads, provider effects, legal/forensic work, failure recovery, benchmark/evolution work, cross-chat heartbeat use, large tool output and visual-artifact assurance.

Synthetic pairs can prove only structural properties: required-control retention, hard-veto behavior, context-size reduction and deterministic non-regression fixtures. They cannot prove model-answer quality, browser responsiveness, provider runtime behavior, owner-time savings or stable-release fitness.

## Promotion ladder

1. `MEASUREMENT_HOLD` — one or more required controls/behaviors fail, or context reduction is below the configured floor.
2. `STRUCTURAL_CANDIDATE` — every synthetic/observed pair passes with zero required-control or behavior omissions and at least 80% median active-context reduction.
3. `EMPIRICAL_VALUE_CANDIDATE` — structural gate plus at least 30 proof-referenced observed paired runs with non-worse median tool round trips and owner interventions.
4. Stable SLOS promotion is explicitly outside this court and remains false even at stage 3.

## Cold-history rule

A compiled hot path may select and reference canonical history, but it may not delete or replace replayable source, event, proof, failure or lineage records. Hashes and capsules are pointers/projections, not substitutes for cold evidence.

## Rollback

Discard the branch/PR. Stable SLOS v0.7.0, canonical doctrine, KDV, provider state and external runtimes are untouched by this measurement candidate.
