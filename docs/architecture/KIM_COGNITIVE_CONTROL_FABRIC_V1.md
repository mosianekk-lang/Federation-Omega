# Kim Cognitive Control Fabric v1

Status: SOURCE_CANDIDATE  
Architecture: Cognitive Policy Market -> Counterfactual Twin -> SOL 6.2 Constitutional Admission  
Authority: proposal/admission only; no provider effect authority

## Purpose

BCΩ-PRIME should not be merged into SOL 6.2. The stronger design separates exploratory intelligence from transactional reality while allowing deep, evidence-bound cooperation.

- **BCΩ-PRIME** is the lead meta-executive and strategy composer.
- **CFBE / Forest / Horizon / Omega Scientist / adversarial and causal twins** may provide independent policy proposals and challenges.
- **The Cognitive Policy Market** ranks candidate policies using quality, evidence, reliability, reversibility, information gain, cost, owner burden and risk.
- **The Counterfactual Twin** stress-tests candidates against deterministic provider-loss, evidence-shock, resource-spike and contradiction-surge scenarios. These are decision-support scenarios, not observed provider truth.
- **SOL 6.2** is the constitutional runtime. It may accept, constrain, defer, reject, seek evidence, or require owner authority. It remains independently operable if the entire cognitive market disappears.
- **Bubbles** remains the elastic workforce/orchestration fabric.
- **SOVARA** remains the provider/model/substrate mesh.
- **ProofOS / independent witnesses** remain the evidence admission plane.

## Non-negotiable invariants

1. **SOL does not import the Cognitive Policy Market.** Dependency direction is one-way: cognitive layers may observe SOL; SOL remains independently operable.
2. **Successful market admission is advisory by default.** Source code or a good counterfactual score cannot promote PRIME into live control.
3. **Bounded topology-control candidacy requires the existing BCΩ-PRIME Value Foundry bridge on the same exact source head.** The bridge must prove hosted-shadow evidence, independently resolved shadow evidence, rollback evidence, at least 30 shadow pairs, at least 30 prospective owner-value pairs and positive owner value. Even then the result is only `CANDIDATE_BOUNDED_TOPOLOGY_CONTROL`.
4. **Policy admission is never dispatch authority.** Every market/admission receipt keeps `dispatch_authorized=false` and `external_effect_authorized=false`.
5. **No intelligence inherits authority.** Provider effects still require SOL fencing, workload identity, gateway policy, action-specific authority and provider readback.
6. **High uncertainty, contradiction, consequential work or an external-effect champion requires independent proposer diversity.** Multiple routes emitted by PRIME alone do not satisfy cognitive diversity.
7. **Counterfactual scores are not provider evidence.** They only stress candidate policies before execution.
8. **Current SOL truth can override a stale PRIME assumption.** If SOL observes authority/provider/runtime loss, the policy is held even if PRIME previously assumed availability.
9. **Verified reality is immutable for new meta-policy.** A mission already closed as `VERIFIED_REALITY` rejects new policy control.
10. **Owner gates remain owner gates.** No policy market can convert an owner-required decision into autonomy.
11. **Consequential effects remain serialized.** Policy topology cannot bypass SOL atomic dispatch/commit boundaries.
12. **Stable self-promotion remains prohibited.** Bounded topology-control candidacy is not stable policy promotion and never implies provider-effect control.

## Cognitive diversity rule

The market distinguishes *route diversity* from *proposer diversity*.

Three PRIME-generated strategies are still one cognitive source. When uncertainty, contradiction or consequence is high, at least one proposal must come from an independent faculty such as Omega Scientist, Adversarial Twin, Causal Twin, Forest Mind, Horizon Mind, CFBE, or the human owner.

This reduces meta-cognitive monoculture without creating another sovereign control plane.

## Counterfactual stress court

Every proposal is evaluated against a bounded deterministic scenario set:

- `BASELINE`
- `PROVIDER_LOSS`
- `EVIDENCE_SHOCK`
- `RESOURCE_SPIKE`
- `CONTRADICTION_SURGE`

The court combines worst-case and mean performance into a robustness score. Policies that fall below the robustness floor can be rejected before SOL execution is considered. Provider-dependent policies are specifically penalized under provider-loss scenarios.

## SOL constitutional verdicts

- **ACCEPT** — advisory policy is admissible; still no dispatch authority.
- **CONSTRAIN** — advisory policy is admissible with reduced parallelism, extra proof or adversarial validation.
- **DEFER** — provider runtime or exact action authority is unavailable.
- **REJECT** — objective/mission mismatch, verified-reality closure, or insufficient counterfactual robustness.
- **SEEK_EVIDENCE** — evidence coverage or independent challenger diversity is insufficient.
- **OWNER_REQUIRED** — an exact human decision remains mandatory.

## Failure relationship

- If PRIME / the market fails, **SOL continues safely** with its ordinary mission/transition contracts.
- If SOL is unavailable, PRIME may reason and emit proposals, but **nothing consequential becomes reality**.
- If SOVARA/provider runtime is unavailable, provider-dependent policy is deferred while safe non-provider lanes may remain executable.

## Explicit non-claims

This architecture does not establish provider-live autonomy, continuous background execution, universal exactly-once behavior, market superiority, stable self-improvement, external effect authority or production cutover. Those remain separate empirical and authority gates.
