# Kim Cognitive Control Fabric v1

Status: SOURCE_CANDIDATE  
Architecture: Cognitive Policy Market -> Counterfactual Twin -> SOL 6.2 Constitutional Admission  
Authority: proposal/admission only; no provider effect authority

## Purpose

The Federation must not create a more powerful monolithic brain by merging BCΩ-PRIME into SOL 6.2. The stronger architecture separates exploratory intelligence from transactional reality.

- **BCΩ-PRIME** is the lead meta-executive and strategy composer.
- **CFBE / Forest / Horizon / Scientist / adversarial and causal twins** may provide independent policy proposals and challenges.
- **The Cognitive Policy Market** ranks proposals using explicit quality, evidence, reliability, reversibility, information gain, cost, burden and risk.
- **The Counterfactual Twin** stress-tests candidate policies under provider loss, evidence shock, resource pressure and contradiction surge. These are deterministic decision-support scenarios, not observed provider truth.
- **SOL 6.2** is the constitutional runtime. It may accept, constrain, defer, reject, seek evidence, or require owner authority. It remains independently operable if the entire cognitive market is absent.
- **Bubbles** remains the workforce/orchestration fabric.
- **SOVARA** remains the provider/model/substrate mesh.
- **ProofOS / independent witnesses** remain the evidence admission plane.

## Non-negotiable invariants

1. **SOL does not import the Cognitive Policy Market.** Dependency direction is one-way: cognitive layers may observe SOL; SOL remains independently operable.
2. **Policy admission is not dispatch authority.** Every market/admission receipt keeps `dispatch_authorized=false` and `external_effect_authorized=false`.
3. **No intelligence inherits authority.** Provider effects still require SOL fencing, workload identity, gateway policy, action-specific authority and provider readback.
4. **High uncertainty, contradiction, consequential work or an external-effect champion requires independent proposer diversity.** Multiple routes emitted by PRIME alone do not satisfy cognitive diversity.
5. **Counterfactual scores are not provider evidence.** They only stress candidate policies before execution.
6. **Current SOL truth can override a stale PRIME assumption.** If SOL observes authority/provider/runtime loss, the policy is held even if PRIME previously assumed availability.
7. **Verified reality is immutable for new meta-policy.** A mission already closed as `VERIFIED_REALITY` rejects new policy control.
8. **Owner gates remain owner gates.** No policy market may convert an owner-required decision into autonomy.
9. **Consequential effects remain serialized.** Policy topology cannot bypass SOL's atomic dispatch/commit boundaries.
10. **Stable promotion remains empirical.** Shadow/advisory/bounded-control maturity must be earned with paired cases, independent verification, rollback, owner-value evidence and regression controls.

## Cognitive diversity rule

The market distinguishes *route diversity* from *proposer diversity*.

Three PRIME-generated strategies are still one cognitive source. When uncertainty or consequence is high, at least one proposal must come from an independent faculty such as Omega Scientist, Adversarial Twin, Causal Twin, Forest Mind, Horizon Mind, CFBE, or the human owner.

This reduces meta-cognitive monoculture without creating another sovereign control plane.

## Counterfactual stress court

Every proposal is evaluated against a bounded deterministic scenario set:

- `BASELINE`
- `PROVIDER_LOSS`
- `EVIDENCE_SHOCK`
- `RESOURCE_SPIKE`
- `CONTRADICTION_SURGE`

The court calculates a worst-case/mean robustness score. A failed robustness floor can reject a policy before SOL execution is even considered. Provider-dependent policies are explicitly penalized under provider-loss scenarios.

## SOL constitutional verdicts

- **ACCEPT** — policy control is admissible; still no dispatch authority.
- **CONSTRAIN** — policy is admissible with reduced parallelism, extra proof or adversarial validation.
- **DEFER** — provider runtime or exact action authority is unavailable.
- **REJECT** — objective/mission mismatch, verified-reality closure, or insufficient counterfactual robustness.
- **SEEK_EVIDENCE** — evidence coverage or independent challenger diversity is insufficient.
- **OWNER_REQUIRED** — an exact human decision remains mandatory.

## Failure relationship

The failure relationship is intentionally asymmetric:

- If PRIME / the market fails, **SOL can continue safely** with its normal mission/transition contracts.
- If SOL is unavailable, PRIME may reason and emit proposals, but **nothing consequential becomes reality**.
- If SOVARA/provider runtime is unavailable, provider-dependent policy is deferred while safe non-provider lanes may remain executable.
