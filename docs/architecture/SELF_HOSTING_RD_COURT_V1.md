# SELF_HOSTING_RD_COURT_V1

## Purpose

SELF_HOSTING_RD_COURT_V1 makes the existing FUSE development fabric itself challengeable without creating another sovereign controller, scheduler, source authority, memory root, or Judge.

**Optimize means; never redefine the owner's end.**

The court reuses Forge, Hypercube, FPPRE, SICF, AECL, Work Plane, FDOF and the independent Reality Judge.

## Clean-room and authority boundary

Development may use owner-controlled source, public mechanisms and authorized interfaces. The court does not authorize bypassing credentials, access controls, private APIs, platform policy, or provider authority boundaries.

## Terminal court

Promotion requires all applicable predicates:

1. Exact source currentness.
2. Reproducible build.
3. Challenger ready.
4. Matched incumbent/challenger evaluation.
5. Quality nonnegative.
6. Dependency exit.
7. **Disconnected rebuild** from owner-controlled assets.
8. Rollback ready.
9. Owner-value gain.
10. **Independent Judge** acknowledgement.

## Maturity separation

**Control deployment is not source admission.**

**Source admission is not runtime proof.**

**Runtime proof is not value proof.**

The builder cannot self-promote. FDOF remains the source critical-section authority, and later provider or production effects require their own explicit authority and proof.

## Self-hosting objective

The incumbent development fabric should eventually be able to produce a bounded challenger development fabric, while the challenger can reproduce the incumbent from canonical owned inputs. Cross-build, recovery, rollback and disconnected rebuild courts are stronger evidence than simple code-generation success.
