# FIO-Ω / SIR-Ω v1.5 — SOVARA-Derived Kim Dataverse Surface Access Fabric

## Objective

Give FIO-Ω/SIR-Ω complete governed visibility over every current and future Kim Dataverse surface, plus automatic use of safe current routes, without converting the intelligence substrate into a universal credential, provider superuser, or parallel authority plane.

## Reused SOVARA laws

FIO reuses rather than duplicates SOVARA's access doctrine:

1. SOVARA remains mission/route/effect admission authority.
2. Provider/surface cells are isolated failure domains.
3. One held provider never globally stalls independent eligible routes.
4. Authority, health, credentials and maturity never inherit across cells.
5. Credentials remain opaque mission-scoped references; raw secret values do not enter FIO state.
6. Safe A0/A1 work may continue automatically when current semantic readback exists.
7. External effects require exact action authority, target, idempotency, provider-native semantic readback and rollback when reversible.
8. Stale proof downgrades the route before the next effect.
9. New surfaces auto-enroll read-only/shadow until independently proven.
10. Transport success is never completion.

## Automation semantics

`AUTO` means the system performs work without another owner prompt only when all of the following are true:

- the mission already authorizes the action class;
- the surface is present and fresh;
- the exact capability is exposed;
- requested authority is within that surface's ceiling;
- semantic readback is available;
- the action is A0/A1 internal/safe or is handed to SOVARA for exact A2 admission.

It does **not** mean unrestricted provider mutation.

Standing communication rule: Gmail/Outlook email sending remains explicit-user-directive-only. Similar owner/public/security/financial effects are action-specific and never inferred from general FIO automation.

## Source contract

`federation/fio_surface_access.py` is deterministic and effect-free. It may decide:

- `AUTO_ROUTE_SAFE_INTERNAL`
- `DISCOVERED_READ_ONLY_SHADOW`
- `SURFACE_STALE_DOWNGRADED`
- `SURFACE_*_HELD`
- `DELEGATE_TO_SOVARA`
- `SOVARA_PREFLIGHT_HELD`
- `EXPLICIT_OWNER_DIRECTIVE_REQUIRED`
