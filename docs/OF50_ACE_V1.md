## B1 — Mission sovereignty

Every material cycle has a versioned Human Mission Contract. Objective and required outcomes are hash-bound into Completion Escrow. Friction may change route; it may not silently rewrite the objective or waive required outcomes. The continuation latch remains engaged until a valid terminal state or exact owner-reserved boundary is reached.

## B2 — Friction immunity

Friction is typed rather than treated as a generic blocker. Transient/tool/executor/dependency/coordination/stale-state/schema/provider/latency/implementation friction is routed around or repaired. Exact authority, safety, irreversible-consequence and true external-unavailability boundaries may hold only their affected predicates. P0/P1 route portfolios require materially distinct failure domains rather than cosmetic route labels.

## B3 — Completion dominance

Proof-carrying actions must target terminal predicates, have positive expected predicate delta, remain within authority and carry rollback/readback targets. Safe machine-resolvable debt blocks completion. Zero-delta action families are circuit-broken and unchanged failed routes require changed predicates or changed routes.

## B4 — Directive fulfillment

A Deliverable Contract preserves requested fruit, sequencing and response obligations. Adjacent work, tool activity or orchestration does not satisfy an omitted deliverable. Completion requires every required deliverable to be satisfied, exactly blocked, or explicitly superseded by the owner.

## B5 — Truth-proof

Material claims are classified by consequence. Evidence precedence, independence, freshness and provider/system provenance govern verification. Self-attestation cannot verify itself. Negative claims such as “unavailable” require route discovery first: `UNKNOWN` must not be promoted to `UNAVAILABLE` without evidence.

## B6 — Claim-source protection

Repeatedly contradicted claim families can be quarantined until a reusable prevention mechanism is proven. Quarantined sources cannot close completion predicates. Requalification is evidence-bound rather than narrative.

## B7 — Provider verifier mesh

Provider verifier status requires executor callability, provider invocation, substantive response, packet identity, falsifier analysis, provider execution evidence, semantic readback and independence. Account access, installation, authentication, connectivity, source presence or model agreement cannot be promoted to model execution.

## B8 — Human-First

Human-First terminal controls preserve intent fidelity, owner-reserved decisions, privacy, reversibility, interruption and cognitive budgets. Avoidable machine-resolvable manual work cannot be offloaded to the owner. Where Human-First is part of the active mission, its terminal predicates must close before completion.

## Terminal-predicate interlock

Before action selection, a material mission may compile required completion conditions into explicit terminal predicates. Each predicate records desired/current state, proof requirement, terminal action, required execution surface, dependencies and authority ceiling. If no qualified executor exists, the critical path becomes executor acquisition: discover surfaces → verify exact identity/authority → run bounded no-effect callability → require action-specific readback → then perform the terminal action.

Control metadata, queues, schedules, source, CI, heartbeats and generic HTTP facts do not count as provider execution. Expected terminal-predicate delta outranks convenience. Progress narration is allowed only when a predicate changes, a previously impossible terminal action becomes callable, or material evidence changes the optimal route.
