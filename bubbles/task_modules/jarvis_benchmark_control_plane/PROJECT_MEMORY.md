# Project memory

## Canonical objective

Maintain a current, evidence-linked view of JARVIS readiness; compare it with the strongest publicly verifiable standards; identify the highest-utility gap; learn from every verified delta; and preserve the boundary between recommendation and authorized action.

## Durable rules

1. Public primary evidence may establish a benchmark target. Private internal-team performance may not be inferred.
2. Source freshness is part of truth. Critical staleness disables current-best conclusions.
3. Maturity assertions are proof-weighted. `TESTED` is not `DEPLOYED`; `DEPLOYED` is not `PROVEN`.
4. The learning ledger is append-only and hash-chained. Repair means recovery from a verified checkpoint, never silent history rewriting.
5. At most three opportunities are surfaced; one Formation-authorized route may become effectful.
6. Continuous operation is not claimed until a scheduler and deployment are authorized and read back.
7. No avoidable manual task, recurring cost, security weakening or workflow allowlist expansion is permitted.

## Current build state

Designed and implemented locally. Test and contract receipts are written into `proofs/` after the verification cycle. Deployment, production authorization and independent proof remain separate states.
