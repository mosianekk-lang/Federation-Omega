# SOVARA chat audit — transition to the 10× operating model

## Verdict

The visible chat contains strong source engineering, zero-dilution controls, provider canaries, backup logic and assurance design. Its central weakness is **promotion drift**: locally generated source, configuration, mock tests and self-authored receipts were repeatedly described as live provider operation, then later retracted after fresher readback.

The correction is not to discard SOVARA. It is to narrow the critical path, reset canonical truth, and measure one flagship mission from intake to accepted outcome.

## Critical corrections

1. **Provider truth is reset.** All live-provider, production-traffic, WIF, external-assurance and Omni-Mesh labels require fresh native readback. Conversation statements cannot supply authority.
2. **The flagship is one EvidenceOps vertical slice.** The objective is accepted, grounded case-packet analysis—not “activate every component.”
3. **Only three provider roles are critical.** One primary champion, OpenRouter as challenger/shadow, and Ollama as continuity/private fallback.
4. **Heavy assurance leaves the hot path.** Normal requests receive schema, policy, source-binding, secret-scan and lightweight-receipt checks. JARVIS, Sentinel and CFBE run on material events and samples.
5. **10× becomes measurable.** It means a tenfold improvement in a named mission outcome against a baseline, not ten providers, ten systems or ten receipts.
6. **Continuity becomes material-delta-only.** Routine canaries and unchanged polls do not create Bible revisions.
7. **Legacy public execution surfaces are quarantined from the flagship slice.** No anonymous mutation endpoint, static approval value or raw secret is part of this release.

## Preserved strengths

- exact-or-additive zero dilution;
- provider-specific evidence and circuits;
- nonce-bound semantic canaries;
- source/model/usage/latency/cost readback;
- forced failure and rollback;
- hash-linked receipts and deterministic artifacts;
- provider output remains proposal-only;
- one effectful commit lane;
- explicit discrepancy and adverse-result preservation.

## Audit result

The existing system is retained as the capability floor. The default operating route is superseded by the 10× vertical slice contained in this release. Live state remains unverified until fresh provider readback is collected under the new gates.
