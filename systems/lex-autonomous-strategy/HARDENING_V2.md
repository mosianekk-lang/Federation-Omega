# LEX Hardening v2 — Cross-Engine Matter Release Court

## Purpose

LEX Hardening v2 is an additive, read-only composition layer. It does not replace or fork existing legal/evidence/foresight engines.

## Reuse boundaries

- LEX-OMEGA owns legal proposition/authority convergence and current-authority semantics.
- TruthGrid owns evidentiary finality/decision readiness.
- JFRIE owns fail-closed release integrity.
- CASEFORGE/SCIENTIA own independent falsification/evolution.
- LASE owns autonomous internal strategy selection.
- HORIZON-LEX owns adaptive consequential foresight.

The new `LexMatterReleaseCourtV2` consumes explicit proof states from those owners and adds only structural controls that were not first-class in LASE: decision-record completeness, chronology/decision-evidence sequence, remedy prerequisites, opponent/tribunal completeness, information-gain prioritisation, capability maturity separation and Failure-to-Operational-Win non-self-promotion.

## Release semantics

The court returns only:

- `INTERNAL_WORK_PRODUCT_ELIGIBLE`, or
- `HOLD`.

It always reports `external_effect=false` and `consequential_actions_owner_reserved=true`. It does not send, file, serve, mutate evidence, create legal authority or promote provider/runtime maturity.

## Proof obligations

Source presence, deterministic tests, runtime proof, provider proof, behavioural proof and owner-value proof remain distinct. No stage inherits a higher stage. Current law and matter-bound AUTHSEM proof remain mandatory upstream inputs for consequential legal work.

## Failure-first regressions

The focused court covers upstream LEX/JFRIE/TruthGrid/CASEFORGE failure, stale/unbound AUTHSEM or current-law state, effectful LASE runs, incomplete decision records, impossible temporal/decision-evidence sequences, remedy gaps, weak opponent/tribunal preparation, capability maturity confusion and self-promotion attempts.

## Maturity boundary

Repository source admission and deterministic regression success do not prove live matter behaviour or owner-value superiority. The next maturity gates are a no-effect shadow on real matter control-state and prospective matched-case evaluation before any broader promotion.
